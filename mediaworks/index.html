How a show comes together

Plan. Capture. Polish. Publish.

  1. Plan

    We learn the show, the audience and the platforms, then lock the look and the schedule.

  2. Capture

    On location, in studio or fully remote. Cameras, audio and a tested backup plan.

  3. Polish

    Editing, audio cleanup, graphics, captions and clips, with your notes built in.

  4. Publish

    Titles, thumbnails and descriptions, uploaded and scheduled everywhere it belongs.

  5. Grow

    Keep it rolling every week, and see in plain numbers what's working.
