Straight answers

Common questions

Don't see yours? Ask. There are no dumb questions about technology.

Do I have to replace my software?

No. We start with the tools you already pay for. New software only when it clearly earns its place.

Will anything get sent without my approval?

Not in the workflows we build. The tools prepare the draft; a person decides what goes out. Any deeper automation happens only with your say-so, tested first.

Is this going to replace my people?

No. The point is to take the retyping, searching and copy-paste off their plate so they can do the work only people can do.

What about sensitive data?

We agree up front what data goes where. Anything sensitive gets its own handling plan before work starts.

How much does it cost?

Every job is scoped and priced before work starts, so there are no surprise invoices. The first conversation is just a conversation.
